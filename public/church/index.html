Who We Are

More Than a Church —
A Global Family

The Seventh-day Adventist Church is one of the world's fastest-growing Christian denominations, united by a common faith and a shared mission to proclaim the everlasting gospel to every nation, kindred, tongue, and people.

Christ-Centered Worship

Every service is designed to draw us closer to Jesus through prayer, Scripture, and Spirit-filled praise.

Wholistic Community

We care for the whole person — spiritual, physical, and social — through health, education, and social outreach.

Global Mission

Operating in over 200 countries, we work together to share hope with communities in every corner of the earth.
