About Barina

Built in Nakuru.
Designed for the Future.

Barina Community is a visionary master-planned settlement located on the fertile highlands of Nakuru, Kenya's fourth-largest city and capital of the Rift Valley region. At 1,850 metres above sea level, Nakuru enjoys a near-perfect highland climate — cool mornings, warm sunny days, and cool evenings — making it one of the most liveable cities in East Africa.

Adjacent to the world-famous Lake Nakuru National Park — home to flamingoes, rhinos, lions, and over 400 bird species — Barina offers residents a lifestyle that is as aspirational as it is grounded in community. We are building more than homes; we are building a legacy.
