About the Project

More Than a Home —
A Complete Way of Life

Barina Community is a visionary planned settlement in Kenya, designed to be entirely self-sustaining. From organic food production to world-class healthcare, education, and commerce — everything your family needs is within walking distance.

Since our founding, we have set out to create not just houses, but an entire ecosystem where families thrive. Members enjoy transparent governance, shared prosperity, and a genuine community spirit built on mutual commitment.

Our Mission

To develop an inclusive, self-reliant community where members enjoy premium living, food security, quality health, and economic opportunity — all in one planned space.

Our Vision

To be East Africa's most admired planned community — a model of sustainable living, modern infrastructure, and diverse, thriving membership by 2035.

Community Values

Integrity, transparency, mutual support, environmental stewardship, and inclusive governance form the bedrock of the Barina Community charter.

Sustainability

Solar micro-grid, rainwater harvesting, organic farming, and green building standards ensure Barina is a net-positive ecological community.
