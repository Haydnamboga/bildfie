    About Barina

    More Than a Home —
    A Complete Way of Life

    Barina is a visionary, government-registered planned community in Kenya — fully self-sustaining, with organic food, healthcare, education, commerce, and social life all within a secure, beautifully designed settlement.

    Our Mission

    To create an inclusive, self-reliant community where members enjoy premium living, food security, quality health, and economic opportunity in one planned space.

    Sustainable by Design

    Solar micro-grid, rainwater harvesting, organic farming, and green construction standards ensure Barina is net-positive ecologically and economically.

    Inclusive Community Governance

    Every member participates in community decisions, project tracking, and governance — via the Barina Member Platform, transparent from day one.
