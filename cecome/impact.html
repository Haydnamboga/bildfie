Prevention

200 girls, one promise kept

On a bright morning in Kisii, more than two hundred girls walked into a graduation hall in their best dresses — not to be cut, but to be celebrated. Over a week of teaching they had learned about their bodies, their rights, and the traditions worth keeping.

As families looked on, leaders did something rare: they turned to the men in the room and challenged them, directly, to protect their daughters and end FGM for good. The cut had been replaced — by knowledge, by dignity, and by a community willing to say “no more” out loud.

Wins that change systems

Advocacy that moves more than hearts

Stories change minds; systems change lives. Here is some of what our coalition has helped set in motion.

✓County gender policy — drawing the Gender Sector Working Group into a push for adoption in Kisii.
✓Inter-county dialogue on SGBV legislation, alongside the County Director for Gender.
✓Stronger civil society — CSOs trained in social accountability, forming a county-wide network.
✓Public mobilization — radio & TV campaigns and a petition for a functional rescue centre.
