Make a gift

Your gift, turned into protection

CECOME is rooted in the community, so your support goes a remarkably long way. Here is what a single gift can do.

$25Sponsors one girl through a week of Alternative Rite of Passage teaching.
$120Seeds a village savings group for survivors and mothers.
$300Supports a school mentorship club for a full term.
$500Powers a community radio campaign reaching thousands.

Amounts are illustrative. Gifts of any size make a difference — and recurring gifts help us plan ahead.

For funders & institutions

Partner with a trusted grassroots delivery team

CECOME has delivered alongside the European Union, Solidaarisuus, Equality Now, the YW4A consortium and county government. We combine deep community trust with the accountability and monitoring that serious funders expect.

Partner with us to co-design programmes, fund a thematic area, or strengthen civil society across the Gusii region.

  • Technical & research collaboration
  • Coalition building & sub-granting to local CBOs
